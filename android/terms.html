Effective November 20, 2026

Choreprint helps families keep track of chores and what each child has earned. By using Choreprint for Android, you agree to these terms. Choreprint on iPhone has its own terms.

The short version

  • A record keeper, not a bank. Choreprint never holds or moves money.
  • Parents are in charge. You invite your family and decide when and how to pay.
  • Your content stays yours. Everything you add is stored in your family's private hub.
  • Google Play handles any purchases. Subscriptions are billed and managed in your Google account.

Who provides Choreprint

Choreprint is a Solocosmo app, published by Quintilian LLC ("we," "us").

Family hubs

  • A parent starts a family hub and invites the rest of the family. The parent who starts the hub is responsible for who is invited.
  • Parents are responsible for how their children use the app, including any payment apps children use.
  • Please share invite links, the parent PIN, and grandparents' and caregivers' codes only with your own family.
  • Keep your invite link and the parent PIN somewhere safe. On a new phone they are the way back into your hub, and without them we can't recover it.

Earnings and payments

  • Balances in Choreprint are a record your family keeps. They are not money, a deposit, or a promise of payment by us.
  • Payouts happen outside the app, in Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, in cash, or however your family decides. Parents decide when and whether to pay.
  • Those payment services have their own terms and age rules. Cash App and Venmo accept ages 13 to 17 only through a teen account that a parent sets up or approves in that app, and PayPal requires users to be 18 or older. Choreprint offers only the services a child's age allows once a parent adds it, but each account is set up outside Choreprint, and parents are responsible for following each service's rules.
  • Cash or in person works at any age. The child or a parent can mark a cash payout as paid.
  • We are not responsible for payments, mistakes, or disputes in those services.

Your content

You keep ownership of everything your family adds, such as names, photos, chores, and drawings. Only add photos and drawings you have the right to use. Your content is stored in your family's hub, which only your family can see, and we don't use it for anything but running the app for you.

Using the app fairly

Do not use Choreprint to harass anyone, to break the law, or to try to get into a family hub you were not invited to. Do not try to copy, reverse engineer, or interfere with the app or its servers.

Subscriptions

  • One subscription covers your whole family hub.
  • Free trials and subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play through your Google account.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel. Cancel anytime in Google Play, under Payments and subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policies.
  • If a subscription lapses, nothing is deleted. Chores and money become read-only until someone renews.

Firebase and availability

Choreprint for Android stores and shares your family's hub with Google's Firebase. We can't recover a hub or anything in it once it's deleted, and we can't promise the app will always be available or free of errors. Notifications depend on Firebase and Android too, so they can arrive late, or only after Choreprint is opened again.
