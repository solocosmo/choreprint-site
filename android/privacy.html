Sheet P-2, Android

Privacy Policy for Android

Effective November 19, 2026

How Choreprint for Android handles your family's information, in plain language. Choreprint on iPhone keeps each family's hub in its own iCloud and has its own policy.

The short version

  • Your hub is private. Only the people you invite can see what's in it.
  • It's stored on Google's Firebase servers, in a database Choreprint runs for families on Android.
  • No ads, tracking, or analytics. Nothing about your family is sold or shared.
  • Money never moves through the app. Payouts happen in the payment app you already use.

Who we are

Choreprint is a Solocosmo app, published by Quintilian LLC ("we," "us").

What your family adds

To work, Choreprint keeps what your family enters:

  • First names, avatars, and any profile photos you choose to add
  • The names each child uses for each grown-up
  • Usernames for Cash App, Venmo, or PayPal, if you add them
  • Each child's age and birthday (month and day), if a parent adds them
  • Which children take turns on a grown-up's phone
  • Chores, allowances, questions and answers, chore ideas, earnings, cash-out requests, and payment records
  • Your hub's name, your home picture, and hub settings such as the cash-out minimum
  • The parent PIN and each grandparent's or caregiver's code, stored only as a scrambled code, never as the PIN itself

Signing in

  • Parents sign in with Google, so the hub is never lost with a phone. Google tells Choreprint the account's email address, name, and an account ID, which are used only to sign you back in to your hub.
  • Children, grandparents, and caregivers join from the invite link with no email and no account to make. Each of their phones gets a private sign-in ID instead.

Where it is stored

  • Your family's hub is stored in Choreprint's Firebase project, a Google Cloud service, on servers in the United States.
  • It travels encrypted between your phones and Firebase, and Google encrypts it where it is stored.
  • A few preferences stay only on each phone, such as text size, which chore sections are folded away, and which notifications that phone gets.

Who can see it

  • Only the people in your hub can see what's in it. The database checks every request against your hub's member list.
  • Quintilian LLC runs the Firebase project, so we have administrator access to it. We don't look at your family's information, and we never sell or share it. We would look only to help with a problem you ask us about, or if the law requires it.
  • Google runs the servers for us and handles the data under its Firebase terms. It doesn't use your family's information for ads.

Invite links

Family members join with an invite link. A parent can remove anyone from the hub at any time.

Anyone who has the link can ask to join your hub, so send it only to your family. A parent's spot takes the parent PIN, and a grandparent's or caregiver's spot takes the code in their invite.

Photos and the camera

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to take or pick a photo, such as an avatar or a home picture. Photos are processed on your phone, saved small, and stored with the rest of your family's hub.

Notifications

If you allow them, Choreprint can let you know about things like a new special chore or a cash-out request. When something in your hub changes, our server sends each phone in the hub a silent signal through Firebase Cloud Messaging. The signal carries none of your family's information. The app then checks your hub and writes the notification on the phone itself, so the words never pass through us or anyone else.

To send those signals, the hub keeps an ID for each phone (a notification token). It is removed when that phone leaves the hub. Reminders, such as "Did you do it?" after a child grabs a chore, and birthday notices are set on the phone itself.

Each person picks which notifications they get in Settings, and you can turn them off at any time in Android Settings.

Payments

Choreprint never handles money and never sees bank or card details. Tapping a pay or request button opens Cash App, Venmo, or PayPal with the username, amount, and a short note filled in. What happens next is up to that service and its own privacy policy. Families can also pay in cash or in person and mark it paid in the app.

Purchases

If you buy a subscription, Google Play handles the purchase through your Google account. We never see your payment details. Choreprint saves the date your family's subscription runs through, so everyone in the hub is covered.

Children

Choreprint is made for families, and children use it with a parent's setup and supervision.

  • A parent starts the hub and adds each child. Children join from that parent's invite link, with no email or account.
  • What children add stays inside the family hub: the chores they claim and finish, the questions they ask about chores, and the chore ideas they send.
  • A child's age and birthday, if a parent adds them, are used only to show the payout options that fit that age and to tell parents when a new one opens.
  • We don't ask children for an email, phone number, or location, we show no ads, and nothing a child adds is shared with anyone outside the hub.
  • Parents can see, change, or remove a child's profile at any time, and can delete the whole hub.

Deleting your information

  • A parent can remove any family member from the Family page. A child's chores, questions, chore ideas, and money history are deleted with them, and so is their sign-in account.
  • Anyone who joined can leave the hub from Settings. Children need a parent's PIN to leave.
  • The parent who started the hub can delete it from Settings, which permanently erases everything in it for everyone, along with everyone's sign-in accounts.
  • You can also ask us to delete your information without the app: see choreprint.com/android/delete.html.
  • Deleting the app removes the preferences stored on that phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
