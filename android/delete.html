Sheet D-1, Android

Delete your account

Effective November 20, 2026

For Choreprint for Android. You can delete your information right in the app, or ask us to do it.

On this sheet

  1. In the app
  2. Without the app
  3. What's kept

In the app

  • Leave the hub: Settings > Leave the hub. Your profile comes out of the hub and this phone's private ID is deleted. For a child, their chores, questions, chore ideas, and money history are deleted too. Children need a parent's PIN to leave.
  • Remove someone: a parent can open Family, tap the person, and remove them. Everything above is deleted for them.
  • Delete the whole hub: the parent who started the hub can go to Settings > Delete the hub. This erases every chore, balance, question, chore idea, and photo for everyone, along with every phone's private ID. It can't be undone.

Without the app

Write to hello@choreprint.com with your hub's name, its invite code (the letters after code= in your family's invite link), and which person to delete, or that the whole hub should go. We'll delete it within 30 days and write back when it's done.

What's kept

Nothing about the person stays in the hub once they're deleted. A grown-up's chores and the family's history stay with the family when a grown-up leaves, since the rest of the family still uses them. Google Play keeps its own record of any subscription purchase, under Google's policies.

Questions about deleting?

Send us a note anytime.
